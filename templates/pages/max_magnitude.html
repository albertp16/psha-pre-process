Maximum Magnitude Estimation

Statistical Mmax via the Kijko–Sellevoll estimator (Kijko 2004, Eqs. 6–8) for the doubly truncated Gutenberg–Richter distribution, plus the observed maximum and cumulative moment release. Input should be a declustered catalogue complete above Mmin.

Or upload a declustered catalog (.csv, .hmtk) to override the default
