Moment Magnitude (Mw) — Step 1: Homogenization

Convert the mixed-scale catalogue to one consistent moment-magnitude scale before any analysis (BBS Sec. 3.3.5, p. 69). Per event: reported Mw > Ms→Mw > mb→Mw (Scordilis 2006, cited via Lamessa et al. 2019, p. 5), each relation applied only inside its validity range — the largest events are never extrapolated; Ml-only events are kept as reported and flagged (no folder-backed Ml→Mw relation).
